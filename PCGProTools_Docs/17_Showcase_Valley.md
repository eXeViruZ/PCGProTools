# 17 — Showcase Valley

Showcase Valley is an example level that ships inside PCG Pro Tools by Hanke Interactive. It shows the plugin's PCG nodes and templates working together in one environment: forest, woodland, meadow, orchard, roadside planting, rocky slopes and a deliberately failing diagnostics example.

## Open the map

1. In the Content Browser, open **Settings** and enable **Show Plugin Content**.
2. Open the PCG Pro Tools plugin content folder (mount point `/PCGProKit`) and go to `maps`.
3. Open **Showcase_Valley** (`/PCGProKit/maps/Showcase_Valley`).

The level opens with its generated vegetation already saved. The PCG volumes use **Generate On Load**, so you do not need to press Generate.

Supporting assets (graphs, meshes, materials, terrain texture) are in `/PCGProKit/maps/Showcase_Valley_Assets/`.

## What is in the level

| Item | Count |
|---|---|
| Landscape | about 352.8 × 352.8 m, surrounded by distant terrain and mountain meshes |
| PCG volumes | 14 |
| Showcase PCG graphs | 12 (three woodland volumes share one graph) |
| Cameras | 7 views, each as a horizontal (16:9) camera `CAM_SV_01`–`CAM_SV_07` and a vertical (9:16) camera with the suffix `_9x16` |

## What PCG Pro Tools does in this level

All vegetation, rocks and ground cover inside the 14 PCG volumes is placed by PCG graphs. Each showcase graph starts from a PCG Pro Tools template and uses PCG Pro Tools nodes together with Unreal's own PCG nodes.

| Volume | Graph | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `PCGV_01_DenseForest_ForestSetup` | `PCG_SV_Forest` | Layered forest: canopy, secondary layer, floor rocks; road clearance; the cabin cut out as an exclusion zone (actor tag `PCGExclude`) |
| `PCGV_02_ForestFloor_ClumpScatter` | `PCG_SV_ForestFloor` | Understory in natural clumps (Clump Scatter) |
| `PCGV_03_Woodland_*` (3 volumes) | `PCG_SV_Woodland` | Mixed woodland and forest edge (Blue Noise Scatter, Relax Points, Noise Mask Filter) |
| `PCGV_04_FlowerIslands_ClumpScatter` | `PCG_SV_FlowerIslands` | Flower islands in the meadow |
| `PCGV_05_Meadow_MultiMeshPerMesh` | `PCG_SV_Meadow` | Multi-Mesh Scatter with settings per mesh |
| `PCGV_06_Roadside_TwoRoads` | `PCG_SV_Roadside` | Roadside planting along both road splines (actor tag `Road`) |
| `PCGV_07_RockyHillside_HillsideVegetation` | `PCG_SV_Rocks` | Boulders, rocks and pines on slopes up to 22° |
| `PCGV_08_SteepRocks_SlopeFilter` | `PCG_SV_SteepRocks` | Scree only on steep ground (Slope Filter, 22°–70°) |
| `PCGV_09_Diagnostics_NoPOI_OnPurpose` | `PCG_SV_DiagnosticsDemo` | Intentional failure, see below |
| `PCGV_10_Orchard_GridSnap` | `PCG_SV_Orchard_GridSnap` | Orchard rows on a 7 m grid (Grid Snap) |
| `PCGV_11_Copse_BoundaryDetect` | `PCG_SV_Copse_BoundaryDetect` | Copse with a shrub margin (Boundary Detect) |
| `PCGV_12_LandmarkClearing_DistanceTag` | `PCG_SV_LandmarkClearing_DistanceTag` | Clearing around the stone cairn (Distance To Nearest Tag, actor tag `Landmark`) |

PCG Pro Tools nodes used in the level: Blue Noise Scatter, Boundary Detect, Clump Scatter, Distance To Nearest Tag, Grid Snap, Height Filter, Instance Variation, Noise Mask Filter, Relax Points, Slope Filter, Spline Avoidance and Spline Offset.

Select a volume and open the **PCG Graph Inspector** to see its graph, node settings, **Setup Check** and **Generation Report**. Each graph's description explains what it demonstrates and which inputs or actor tags it needs.

## Environment support content

The following parts of the level are hand-built environment content. They were not generated by PCG Pro Tools; they are there to give the PCG examples a setting:

- the landscape and its terrain material, the distant terrain and the mountain range,
- the roads and the road junction (meshes), plus the two road splines tagged `Road` that the graphs read,
- the cabin and its props, fences, signposts, benches and the stone cairn,
- hand-placed boulders and cliffs (tagged `RockLandmark`, cut out by the rock graphs),
- the information boards and the in-game diagnostics sign,
- the original meshes (trees, bushes, rocks, flowers and props) that the graphs place. They were made for this showcase in Blender.

## The intentional Diagnostics example

`PCGV_09_Diagnostics_NoPOI_OnPurpose` produces **0 points on purpose**. Its graph looks for actors tagged `POI`, and the level contains none. A sign next to the volume says so.

1. Select the volume and open the **Generation Report** in the PCG Graph Inspector.
2. The **Setup Check** section names the missing `POI` tag (two warnings). The **Generation Report** below it shows 0 points and the node warnings from Distance To Nearest Tag and the Attribute Filter. The same two node warnings appear in the log when you regenerate the volume. They are expected.
3. To see the graph work, save the level into your project, add an actor with the tag `POI` inside the volume and regenerate.

## Plugin content is protected

The showcase map and its graphs are plugin content. The Graph Inspector marks them as a read-only shipped graph, and Reset or Apply Preset first ask you to create an editable project copy.

To customize a graph, select its volume and use **Make Editable Copy** in the PCG Graph Inspector. This creates a copy in your project (`/Game/PCG/`), assigns it to the selected volume and leaves the original unchanged. The copy remembers its template, so **Reset to Template** works on it (see [Editable copies and Reset to Template](14_Editable_Copies_and_Reset_to_Template.md)). To keep a changed level, save it into your own project folder as well.

## Import source files

The showcase meshes and the terrain mask texture ship without their original source files, and their import settings no longer point to a source file. Unreal **Reimport** may therefore ask you to choose a new source file; automatic reimport of these assets is not supported.

## Engine and rendering

- Tested with **Unreal Engine 5.8.4**. Other 5.8 versions were not tested with this map.
- The look of the level was set up with the rendering settings that a new Unreal Engine 5.8 project gets from the New Project dialog (for example Lumen global illumination and reflections). In a project with different rendering settings the level loads and generates the same content, but lighting and tone can look different. This is a project setting, not missing content.

## Graphs at a glance

- 12 graphs in `/PCGProKit/maps/Showcase_Valley_Assets/Graphs/`
- Templates they are based on: Forest Setup, Clump Scatter, Natural Forest Scatter, Roadside Generator, Hillside Vegetation, Slope Filter (Curvature Filter), Grid Snap, Boundary Detect, Distance Tag, plus one Multi-Mesh Scatter with settings per mesh
- The showcase graphs are examples. The shipped templates themselves are unchanged and stay available in the Template Library.
